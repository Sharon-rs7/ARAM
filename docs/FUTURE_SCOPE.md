# ARAM AI — Future Scope & Architectural Expansion

---

## 1. Planned Horizons

1. **Statewide Multi-State Expansion**: Expand taxonomy models and authority directories beyond Tamil Nadu to additional Indian states (Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra).
2. **Additional Indic Language Models**: Extend Faster-Whisper fine-tuning and localized templates to Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, and Bengali.
3. **Dedicated Vector Database**: Migration path from parquet/MongoDB cosine search to high-performance distributed vector databases (Qdrant / pgvector) for sub-10ms retrieval across 10M+ statutory clauses.
4. **WhatsApp & Telephony Integration**: Twilio / WhatsApp Business API integration for offline and feature-phone voice complaint triage.
