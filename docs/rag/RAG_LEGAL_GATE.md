# ARAM AI — Legal Relevance Gate Specification
**Location**: `docs/rag/RAG_LEGAL_GATE.md`

## 1. Cross-Domain Rejection Rules
- Property Queries: Rejects *Merchant Shipping Act*, *Indian Ports Act*, *National Anti-Doping Act*, *Maharashtra Lokayukta Act*.
- Labour Queries: Rejects Property/Municipal, Sugarcane Cess, Maritime acts.
- Consumer Queries: Rejects Maritime, Anti-Doping, Lokayukta, Sugarcane acts.
